Setup needed

Open admin/index.html, then fill in FIREBASE and SUPER_ADMIN at the top of the script.

Admin login

Sign in with your admin email.

Site settings

These change the offer page (index.html) without editing code. Changes show after a page refresh.

Plans

Pick a light accent colour. Text on the lime buttons is dark.

Navbar links

Shown on the offer page, Blog and SNK Business pages. Use a full address for other sites (https://snkbp.com). For pages of this site use index.html, blog.html or index.html#plans. Tick "Button" for a black button like Support.

Blog posts

The picture cards on the Blog and SNK Business pages. Pick the page, edit the cards, then press Save posts.

Admins

Only the super admin can add or remove admins. The new admin signs in with the email and password you set here.
